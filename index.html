Five Lineages of Vishwakarma Community

According to tradition, Lord Vishwakarma had five sons whose descendants form the five branches of the Vishwakarma community. Marriages are traditionally considered acceptable among these lineages.

Manu

Associated with metal and blacksmith craftsmanship.

May (Maya)

Traditionally linked with carpentry and wood craftsmanship.

Tvasta

Known for metal casting and mechanical craftsmanship.

Shilpi

Associated with stone carving and temple architecture.

Daivagya

Traditionally linked with goldsmith and precious metal work.
